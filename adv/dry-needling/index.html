Advanced Therapy

Dry Needling

Fine, sterile needles release tight muscle knots (trigger points) for fast relief from pain and spasm.

BOOK AN APPOINTMENTWHATSAPP US
Dry Needling photo

What is it?

Dry needling uses thin, single-use filament needles inserted into tight bands of muscle. This releases the trigger point, improves blood flow and reduces pain that can also be felt elsewhere (referred pain).

It is always combined with stretching and exercise so the relief lasts.

Benefits

Quick relief from muscle spasm
Improves range of motion
Reduces referred pain
Speeds return to exercise

Not recommended if

Strong needle phobia
Bleeding disorders or blood thinners (discuss first)
Infection or wound at the site
First trimester of pregnancy

What happens in a session

01Assessment & trigger point mapping
02Needling, 10–20 minutes
03Stretching & movement
04Exercise follow-up
