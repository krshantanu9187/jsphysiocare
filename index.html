Pain is not something you have to live with

At JS PHYSIOCARE, every treatment starts with understanding why it hurts. We assess how you move, explain what we find in plain language, and build a plan with clear goals — so you know what each session is for.

With the right combination of hands-on therapy and exercise, you regain strength and range of motion — and get back to work, sport and family without pain holding you back.

Why JS Physiocare

Assessment first

  • Full evaluation: posture, movement, strength and joint testing on day one.
  • Clear diagnosis: what is causing your pain, explained simply.
  • Written plan: goals and an expected number of sessions.
  • Progress checks: re-assessed regularly, plan adjusted.

Sports · Ortho · Neuro · Cardio

  • Sports: injury treatment and return-to-play testing.
  • Ortho: spine, joints, muscles and ligaments.
  • Neuro: stroke, Parkinson’s and nerve conditions.
  • Cardio: heart and lung rehabilitation.

Care where you need it

  • Two clinics: fully equipped treatment rooms in Sector 56 and Sector 62, Gurugram.
  • Home visits: for post-operative, elderly and limited-mobility patients.
  • Women’s health: private pelvic floor, ANC and PNC sessions.
  • Easy booking: online, by phone or on WhatsApp.
